About Diff Society

A community for people who enjoy osu! mapping.

Diff Society brings together people who enjoy creating beatmaps, sharing ideas, collaborating on projects, and learning from one another.

We're not a community of top-ranked mappers or an exclusive group for experienced creators. We're a community of people who enjoy mapping, regardless of skill level or experience. Whether you've been mapping for years, have just started your first project, or have never mapped before but want to learn, you're welcome here.

Our Purpose

Diff Society exists to give people a place to connect through osu! mapping, find opportunities to collaborate, share their work, and explore their creativity.

Whether you're looking for a guest difficulty, searching for collaborators, sharing a work in progress, or figuring out how to create your very first beatmap, our goal is to help you connect with others who share your interest in mapping.

Who Is Diff Society For?

Anyone who enjoys mapping, or wants to give it a try.

You don't need a ranked map, an impressive portfolio, or years of experience to join us. You don't even need to have mapped before.

Maybe you've always wanted to learn how to time a song, create patterns, or turn music you love into a beatmap. Maybe you're looking for people to work with, or maybe you simply enjoy mapping as a hobby.

Whatever your experience level, there's a place for you here. We're all here because we enjoy mapping, and you don't have to be an expert to be part of that.

Be Part of Diff Society

Everyone starts somewhere, and learning something new is always easier when you have people to share the experience with.

Whether you're an experienced mapper, a beginner learning the basics, or someone who's simply curious about getting started, we'd love to have you in the community.

You don't have to be the best mapper to belong. You just have to enjoy mapping.
