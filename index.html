Welcome to Diff Society

A community for people who enjoy mapping.

Diff Society is a community built around a shared passion for osu! mapping. This space was created for mappers to share ideas, talk mapping, collaborate on projects, and find people for guest difficulties.

It's not about being the best mapper or how many ranked maps you have. We believe maps are always more fun with more difficulties, and working together makes creating them even more enjoyable while giving everyone more motivation to finish their projects.

Whether you've been mapping for years or have zero experience and want to create your very first map, you're welcome here. We're all here because we enjoy mapping, and there's always room for one more.
